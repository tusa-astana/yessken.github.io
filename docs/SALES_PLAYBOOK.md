# Sales playbook for first organizers

## Goal

Onboard the first 5–10 organizers, publish authorized real events, and validate discovery/RSVP demand. TUSA does not sell offline tickets or collect admission payments.

## Target audience
- nightlife venues
- local promoters
- event agencies
- clubs and bars
- cultural spaces
- organizers with active Telegram channels

## Core pitch

“We help people in Astana discover your event in Telegram, see its details, and express interest. TUSA provides discovery and RSVP, not ticket checkout.”

## Outreach script

### Message 1: short and direct

“Hi! I’m building TUSA, a Telegram event-discovery app for Astana. We help organizers publish an event page and see discovery/RSVP interest. TUSA doesn’t sell offline tickets or take admission payments. I can set up a free listing for one upcoming event and share the engagement results.”

### Message 2: value-based follow-up

“Events can be hard to find across many channels. TUSA offers a focused event page, Telegram sharing, and free RSVP. We’re testing which digital organizer tools are genuinely useful; would a free listing and a simple engagement snapshot help?”

### Message 3: close

“If you’re interested, I can publish your event this week after confirming the details and your permission. We’ll share a link and review views/RSVP together. There’s no ticket commission or payment commitment.”

## Sales principles
- Speak in outcomes, not in app features.
- Sell event discovery and measurable interest, not bookings or ticket sales.
- Lead with a free, moderated launch listing.
- Keep the ask small and measurable.

## Objection handling

### “We already have channels.”
Answer: “That is exactly why this can help: TUSA gives your event a clean page and a shareable Telegram link. It complements your channels; it does not replace your ticket seller.”

### “We don’t have time.”
Answer: “We can publish the event for you and prepare the sales page. You just provide the details.”

### “What if it doesn’t work?”
Answer: “The first listing is free. We measure views and RSVP interest; we won’t call them ticket sales.”

## Conversion checklist

Before closing an organizer:
- event details are confirmed
- date and venue are clear
- event details and any organizer-stated admission information are confirmed
- organizer agrees to share the event link
- measurement plan is defined

## Success metric

The first signal is a real organizer returning to TUSA and valuing its digital tools. Views and RSVP are not proof of paid admission or TUSA revenue. Any future paid Organizer Pro offer must be digital and paid only in Telegram Stars.
