# Client prospecting: first Astana organizers

## Objective

Build a broad, source-checked directory of Astana hobby organizers, clubs and recurring event providers across multiple categories. TUSA can link visitors to a real current plan and provide free RSVP for community meetups; it does not sell offline tickets or take admission payments. The categories are a portfolio, not a preselected winning niche.

## Best client profile

Prioritize public-facing organizers and clubs who:

- run events at least once per month;
- promote through Telegram or Instagram;
- sell paid admission or paid reservations;
- have a clear venue and date;
- can make a decision without a long procurement process.

Also include free or drop-in activities and publicly listed regular schedules. Do not rank categories by assumed popularity; record the source and date checked, whether an official date is published, and whether the organizer permits a TUSA card.

## Prospect segments

### Priority 1: nightlife

- clubs and bars
- DJs and party promoters
- electronic music collectives
- recurring nightlife series

Offer: free moderated event page, Telegram sharing, and RSVP/interest measurement.

### Priority 2: comedy and culture

- stand-up promoters
- theatre and improv groups
- workshops and creative spaces
- small concert organizers

Offer: clean event information, Telegram sharing, and RSVP/interest measurement.

### Priority 3: venues

- independent halls
- lounges and restaurants with ticketed evenings
- coworking and community spaces
- university and professional communities

Offer: recurring event calendar and an in-Telegram discovery channel.

### Priority 4: hobbies, sports and outdoor activities

- hobby clubs and workshops
- padel and other court sports
- equestrian clubs and guided rides
- running/walking communities and outdoor/seasonal plans

Offer: a TUSA hobby-category card linking to the organizer's current public schedule, with a separate free community RSVP only when there is a specific open plan. TUSA does not reserve a court, lesson, horse, seat, or admission.

## Where to search

Use official/public sources and record only necessary business contact details and source metadata. A search result is a lead, not permission to republish or a verified schedule:

- Telegram channels for Astana events, nightlife, music, comedy, and venues
- Instagram event pages and venue profiles
- 2GIS and Google Maps venue listings
- event posters showing organizer or booking contacts
- public event calendars and community pages
- official club/activity schedules and venue calendars

Search phrases:

- `Астана мероприятия билеты`
- `Астана вечеринки организатор`
- `Астана стендап билеты`
- `Астана концерты сегодня`
- `Астана клуб афиша`
- `Астана DJ party`

## Outreach sequence

### First message

“Здравствуйте! Я [имя], пишу от команды TUSA — Telegram-сервиса для поиска событий, занятий и клубов по хобби в Астане. Нашли вашу публичную страницу/расписание: [ссылка]. Проверяем актуальность [конкретная дата/активность]. Разрешаете ли вы разместить короткую карточку со ссылкой на ваш официальный источник? Публикуем только после вашего подтверждения. TUSA не продаёт офлайн-билеты, не бронирует места и не принимает оплату за вход. Если неактуально или не подходит — никаких действий не нужно.”

Use the same explicit TUSA introduction as the first sentence in WhatsApp, Telegram or Instagram. Verify the business channel before sending; personalize with one concrete public source. Send manually after review, never as bulk automated outreach. If no reply, one respectful follow-up at most; honor requests not to contact again. Instagram DM may not support reliable prefilled text, so copy the reviewed text and open the official profile rather than trying to automate the send.

### Follow-up after two days

“Возвращаюсь по TUSA. Для первых организаторов делаем запуск без оплаты: страница события, ссылка для публикации и базовая статистика переходов. У вас есть ближайшее событие, которое можно протестировать?”

### Qualification questions

1. Какое ближайшее событие и дата?
2. Сколько обычно приходит гостей?
3. Где сейчас продаёте билеты или собираете регистрации?
4. Есть ли Telegram-канал или другой канал аудитории?
5. Нужны ли категории билетов, промокоды или QR check-in?

## 48-hour execution sequence

### First 4 hours

- choose the first 5 prospects above;
- find only their public Instagram, Telegram, website, or venue contact;
- record the source URL and date checked;
- send the personalized opener;
- set status to `Contacted`.

### Hours 4–24

- answer questions within the same day;
- qualify the next event, date, price, and expected attendance;
- create a draft event page for every serious reply;
- offer a short 15-minute setup call or chat-based onboarding;
- set status to `Qualified` or `No response`.

### Hours 24–48

- follow up once with the concrete free pilot offer;
- publish the first approved pilot event;
- give the organizer a shareable Telegram link;
- record views, event opens, shares, and RSVP;
- ask for the next event only after delivering the first result.

## CRM statuses

Use one status per lead:

- `New`: sourced, not contacted
- `Contacted`: first message sent
- `Replied`: any response received
- `Qualified`: event and decision-maker confirmed
- `Pilot`: event being prepared
- `Live`: event published
- `Paid`: organizer paid for a verified digital Organizer Pro subscription in Telegram Stars
- `Repeat`: organizer supplied another event
- `No response`: follow-up needed once
- `Not fit`: no suitable event or channel

The only statuses that matter for first money are `Pilot`, `Live`, `Paid`, and `Repeat`.

## Offer ladder

1. First event: free setup and moderated listing.
2. Pilot: measure views, shares, and RSVP without claiming ticket sales.
3. Organizer Pro: offer a Stars-paid digital subscription only after the actual benefit bundle, price, terms, support, and refund flow are verified.
4. Repeat organizer: retain organizers through useful in-app digital tools; no offline ticket commission or external checkout under the current TUSA policy.

## Qualification score

Add one point for each yes:

- event within the next 30 days;
- recurring event format;
- active audience channel;
- organizer replies quickly;
- recurring event format;
- organizer agrees to share the event link.

Prioritize leads scoring 4 or more.

## Weekly operating rhythm

- Monday: find 10 new leads.
- Tuesday: send first messages.
- Wednesday: follow up and qualify replies.
- Thursday: publish pilot event pages.
- Friday: measure views and checkout starts.
- Sunday: ask successful organizers for the next event.

## Success target

Within the first outreach cycle:

- 30 qualified leads;
- 10 conversations;
- 3 pilot events;
- 1 organizer willing to pay for the next campaign.

Do not buy ads until this direct-sales loop produces a repeatable response.
